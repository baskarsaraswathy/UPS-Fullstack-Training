THE PEACOCK

Our National Bird

1. About Peacock

The peacock is a beautiful bird with colourful feathers. It is the national bird of India. The male peacock has a long tail and spreads its feathers like a fan. It looks very beautiful when it dances during the rainy season.

2. Types of Peafowl

There are three main types of peafowl:

  • Indian Peafowl
  • Green Peafowl
  • Congo Peafowl

3. Food Habits

Peacocks eat different types of food, such as:

  • Seeds and grains
  • Fruits
  • Insects and worms
  • Small reptiles

4. Behaviour

Peacocks are active during the daytime. They usually search for food on the ground and rest in trees at night. They can also fly short distances.

5. Where Do Peacocks Live?

Place Details
Forests They find food and shelter.
Grasslands They search for insects and seeds.
Villages They may be seen near farms and fields.

6. Interesting Facts

  1. The peacock is the national bird of India.
  2. Male peafowl have colourful tail feathers.
  3. Peacocks can fly short distances.
  4. They make loud calls.

7. Protecting Peacocks

We should protect peacocks and their natural habitats. We must not harm birds or destroy the forests where they live.
